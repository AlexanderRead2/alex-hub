Grok Bot feature updates

New Grok Bot features and which bots they help · Updated Tue 24 Nov · Bot

Grok Bot usage audit — 4 Nov 2026

23 Nov · on X
Image Markup on desktop

Open an image in the desktop app, choose Markup, circle an area or add numbered comments, and tell the bot what to change there (swap a sky, remove power lines, pick out one object among several). Good for touching up gift photos and card images, and for screenshots and graphics on this hub.

20–23 Nov · on X
Tailscale: reach MCP servers on your own network

Bots can now connect to MCP servers running on your tailnet: a home NAS, a media server, a small home-made tool or a VPS. Useful if any bot needs files or tools that live at home rather than in the cloud, and cheaper for routine jobs your own machine can run.

23 Nov · on X
Hotel bookings alongside flights

The bot can now book hotels as well as flights, following each provider's own steps and sending you the link when a final purchase can't be confirmed. Handy for planning trips and occasions.

19 Nov · on X
Try On clothes before you buy

Clothing products the bot finds show a Try On button. Add a selfie once (kept privately, can be deleted) and it generates a picture of you wearing the item. Handy for the gifts bot and any clothes shopping.

19–20 Nov · on X
New connectors: Google Ads, Google Analytics, Obsidian

Official Google Ads (campaign reports, keyword research, changes with a preview first) and Google Analytics connectors join Search Console. Obsidian's official connector lets a bot create, search and organise notes in a vault, synced by Obsidian Sync. Analytics could show visits to this hub; Obsidian could be a shared notebook for all the bots.

18 Nov · v1.2.3
Per-tool permissions on each connector

On a connector's page, each tool can be set to Always allow, Auto-review, Needs approval or Blocked. Lets you hard-block risky actions (deleting files, sending, trading) for one bot while letting safe reads run without cards. Useful for the trading, storage clean-up and site-check bots.

18 Nov · v1.2.3
Routines handle sign-outs and remember the chat

When a routine needs an account reconnected, the reconnect card also arrives as a notification, and the routine skips runs until you sign back in instead of failing quietly. Helpers' signed-out connectors show a reconnect card in chat. Routines can now read the earlier conversation in their chat, so daily runs keep context. Helps every bot with a daily routine.

18 Nov · v1.2.3
Tidier memory, connectors before browser, phone uploads fixed

Bots keep separate memories for people and organisations, your profile, standing instructions and projects. Sites with a connector are used through it (or a connect card) before opening the browser. Photos and PDFs sent from the phone now reach the bot's computer, and product searches retry once on a blip. Helps the gift, grocery and health-helper bots.

13–16 Nov · v1.1–1.2
Steadier browser work: clicks land, stuck loops stop, faster start-up

Computer-use clicks no longer drift off target, a bot repeating the same clicks gets stopped, a hung step gives up after 30 seconds, the remote browser starts faster with your sign-ins carried over, and desktop automations type faster. Saved-login fills open the real sign-in page first. Helps every bot that works in a signed-in website: grocery ordering, trading checks and site checks.

13–16 Nov · shopping
Smoother checkouts and deal hunting

Deal Watcher checks other stores for a lower price before you pay. Promo codes it finds go in at checkout. Product results show the percentage off, and a sold-out item has a Notify me restock alert. Checkout contact details are sorted before the order card appears, and declined cards are caught. When a store can't take the saved card, the bot can use one from the Vault. Useful for the grocery and gift bots.

13 Nov · new template
Gift Concierge template

A ready-made setup for a budgeted gift list: ideas, approve-before-buy, and delivery tracking. Gift orders show the recipient, the message and whether the gift arrives in time. Worth comparing against the gift bot's current setup.

13–16 Nov · tasks & routines
Follow-ups build on earlier work, and routines finish cleanly

Follow-ups and corrections now continue the existing task instead of starting over. Routines finish running their code before they report back, and work handed to another bot no longer gets stuck in For Review. The Tasks page has a searchable Closed section. Helps every bot with daily routines.

13 Nov · Google Drive & X
Full Docs/Sheets/Slides editing, plus X draft cards

With Google Drive connected, bots can use every Docs, Sheets and Slides editing tool, which helps with storage clean-up and spreadsheet tables. X posts and X Chat messages now show as draft cards and only go out after you tap Send or Post. Chat uploads over 25 MB, including videos, now reach the bot.

13–15 Nov · usage
Plans stretch further: ~7% more usage, up to ~15% more on routines

The Grok Bot team says harness fixes shipped last week give about 7% more usage overall and up to 15% more on routine runs. Nothing to switch on. Biggest win for the bots that run daily routines (groceries deal watch, trading checks, site checks and this feature scout).

14 Nov · limited test
One-time usage reset in exchange for data sharing (some accounts)

On some accounts, hitting the weekly limit shows a prompt: allow your data to be used and you get a one-time usage reset. It's a test, not on every account, so it may never appear. Worth weighing the privacy trade-off before accepting, since the bots handle shopping and finance logins.

12–13 Nov · Mac app
Mac app uses Messages, Mail, Notes, Reminders, Calendar, Location and Contacts

On a Mac, a Bot can now read and send Messages, search and draft Mail, and use Notes, Reminders, Calendar, Contacts and Location, each with its own Allow switch. Useful for the gifts bot (birthdays from Contacts, occasion reminders), the groceries bot (collection-day reminders and calendar slots) and the work bot (calendar and mail drafts). Needs the Mac app.

11 Nov · Grok Bot 1.0 (v1.0.1)
Bots join video meetings

A Bot can join a Google Meet, Zoom or Microsoft Teams call from a link, answer in the meeting chat and send a summary afterwards. Team, Enterprise and Ultra plans only. Would help Fus with work calls and Rep with prospect calls, if the plan allows.

11 Nov · v1.0.1
Voice speed slider and voice picker

Each Bot's Voice menu has a 0.5x–2x speed slider and a card per voice, also adjustable mid-call. Handy for Fus's voice-led work and any bot you talk to often.

11 Nov · v1.0.1
Check price again on expired order cards

An expired order card now offers Check price again, which gets a fresh card with current prices. Helps Del revive a grocery basket without starting over.

11 Nov · v1.0.1
Quieter routines and bigger files

Turns with nothing to report no longer send a bare "done" message, and files up to 250 MB on a Bot's computer no longer break replies. Less noise from scheduled checks (Cap, Del, Bot) and fewer failures for Pat's storage clean-up.

11 Nov · reported, limited rollout (not yet in changelog)
iMessage texting and message search

Reports say the Bot can now send iMessages for you and search older chats, with an approved-contacts list it can text without asking each time. Needs the iOS Grok Bot app and a Mac that stays online. Could help Gift Bot (occasion texts) and Del (quick household check-ins) if you use iPhone and Mac.

3–4 Nov · v0.78.4 and later
Scheduled reorders and smarter usual orders

A scheduled reorder of a usual list asks for one approval per order showing the checkout total. Rebuilt orders open with what's out of stock and what changed price, and once a cart and total are approved the Bot pays without asking again unless something material changes. Helps Del with regular grocery runs.

5 Nov · v0.79.x
Guided gift requests

Asked to find a gift, the Bot asks for recipient, budget, occasion and deliver-by date, and never orders one that would arrive late. Helps Gift Bot.

5 Nov · v0.79.x
Safer checkout and email attachments

The Bot rechecks shipping and total before ordering, only uses discount codes it was given, and warns when a web page hides instructions for AI agents. Gmail and Outlook draft cards now list attachments and Send includes them. Helps Del, Gift Bot, Fus and Pat.

6 Nov · v0.79.3
Dark or light theme on shared Pages

Shared Pages get a theme switch that remembers the choice, and page reads fall back to a direct fetch instead of the browser. Small win for Tek and anyone publishing Pages.

Feature updates

27 Oct · app 0.78.1
Shopping cards, Buy now and order tracking

Product results now show as cards with photos, rating, price and store; Buy now starts an order you approve, and the bot then tracks it and tells you when it ships or arrives. Shop Pay can also be added to the Vault's Payments tab. Helps the grocery and gift bots compare deals and follow deliveries. Changelog

27 Oct · app 0.78.1
Subscription tools and templates

Bots can look up a subscription's price and renewal date and cancel one (confirming first). New templates: Find unused subscriptions, Watch subscription prices, and Try it, don't pay for it (starts a free trial and cancels or reminds you before it bills). Good fit for the clean-up bot. Changelog

27 Oct · app 0.78.1
More human-like browser typing, and Gmail attachments

The bot's browser now types one character at a time and moves the mouse on every site, which should help bots that log in to shopping, trading and other sites. Gmail drafts and sends can attach files from the bot's computer in more cases, and plain-text drafts open as rich text. Changelog

26 Oct · app 0.77.0
Vault Payments tab and smarter saved entries

The Vault now has a Payments tab where you pick which card bots try first (you still approve every purchase), each entry gets a Hints field telling bots when to use it, and bots can update a saved card or login for you. Helps Del's grocery checkouts and Gift Bot's orders go straight to the right card. Changelog

26 Oct · app 0.77.0
1Password without a shared vault

1Password can now connect without setting up a shared vault: you approve each login in 1Password and it fills the bot's browser without the bot ever seeing the password. Useful for Cap, Joe and Del's site sign-ins. Changelog

26 Oct · app 0.77.0
Deal Watcher and Order Concierge templates

New ready-made bot templates: one watches prices and coupons, the other tracks orders, refunds and returns, and neither buys without your OK. Worth borrowing ideas from for Del's deal tracking and Gift Bot's deliveries. Changelog

26 Oct · app 0.77.0
Multi-question cards, longer approvals, table copy

Bots can ask several questions on one stepped card (with your own answer option), approval cards stay open 30 minutes instead of 10, and chat tables have a copy button for Slack or markdown. Handy for Del's pre-order checks, Joe's tables and Rep's lists. Changelog

24 Oct · app 0.75.8
Bots can set up your Auto-review rules for you

When a bot keeps hitting the same approval card, it can offer to add, change or remove an Auto-review rule on a card you confirm, and Auto-review now takes a second look before asking at all. Useful for Del's regular shop checkouts, Joe's routine trading checks and Cap's site sign-ins, so fewer pauses waiting on you. Changelog

Coming soon · in the 0.77 prerelease
Bots can make phone calls for you

A bot dials a person or business, makes the request, waits on hold, presses keys, and sends back a summary, with a live transcript to watch. Handy for Del (ringing a store about stock), Gift Bot (bookings and orders) and Tek (chasing suppliers). Not a group call with several bots, which still isn't available. Announcement

23 Oct · app 0.75.2 or later
Vault for logins, cards and contact details

Save a login, card or address once in Settings and bots fill it in to sign in or check out. Fewer stalls at sign-in pages for Del, Joe and Cap, and smoother checkouts for Del and Gift Bot. Announcement

23 Oct
Fewer approval cards and email forwarding

Auto-review asks less often for things you asked for in your own words, and emails you ask for go out once it allows them (routine sends still wait as drafts). Bots can also forward mail from their own inbox with images and attachments intact. Helps every bot, especially Gift Bot and Fus. Changelog

22 Oct · rolling out gradually
1Password sign-ins

A bot asks 1Password for a login, Alex approves it in the 1Password app, and 1Password fills it in. The bot never sees the password. Needs 1Password 8.12.42 or later. Helps Joe (Freetrade keeps signing out), Del/Bob (Asda click and collect) and any bot that stalls at a sign-in page on early runs.

22 Oct · rolling out on iOS and Android
Phone apps: Calendar, Reminders, Contacts, Messages, Home

Alex chooses which bot can use which phone app (Android needs app 1.17.0). Helps Tek (launch reminders on the phone calendar), Del/Bob (collection slots and early reminders) and Gift Bot (key dates with reminders ahead). Announcement

21 Oct
Pages: reports, trackers and dashboards

A bot writes a live page it keeps updated, shareable with only you, your team or anyone with the link. A private home for things that shouldn't go on this public site, like Joe's trial table or Pat's storage clean-up, and a fit for Del/Bob's pizza price tracking. Announcement

iPhone Action Button voice chat and call memory

One press starts a voice chat with a chosen bot (iPhone 15 Pro or later, app 1.16+). Ring back within 30 minutes and the bot remembers the last call. Best for the bots Alex talks to by voice. Announcement
